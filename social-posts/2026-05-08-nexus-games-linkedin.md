# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox

I've been thinking about how QA engineers actually sharpen their skills — and most practice environments are too clean, too predictable.

So I built Workshop Chaos Sandbox: a hands-on environment where developers and testers can intentionally break things, hit weird edge cases, and work through unpredictable UI scenarios in a safe space.

It's designed for QA workshops, team training sessions, and anyone who wants to get better at finding the bugs that matter — without waiting for production to surprise you.

The scenarios are interactive, the edge cases are real, and the whole thing is ready to drop into your next testing workshop.

Try it out: https://nexus-games-seven-jade.vercel.app
Source is open: https://github.com/nadvolod/nexus-games

Would love feedback from QA engineers and team leads who run workshops — what scenarios would make this more useful for your team?
