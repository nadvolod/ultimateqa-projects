# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of work, KratosRx is live: https://www.kratosrx.com

The problem we kept hearing: people who genuinely need GLP-1 therapy spend weeks navigating referrals, insurance walls, and pharmacy delays — only to give up before they start.

So we built the path we wished existed. A patient completes their intake online, gets evaluated by a licensed physician, and if appropriate, receives their medication at home. No waiting rooms. No runaround.

The stack is telehealth-powered onboarding, end-to-end care management, and direct-to-door delivery — all connected in one experience. Clinical quality without the institutional friction.

This isn't a hack around the system. Every prescription is physician-issued. We just removed the parts that were never helping anyone.
