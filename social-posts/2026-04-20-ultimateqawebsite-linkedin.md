# LinkedIn — UltimateQA - Quality Assurance Learning Platform

After years of teaching QA and test automation, I wanted to build something that actually reflects how I think about this craft — not just video dumps, but structured paths that take you from zero to writing real automation code.

UltimateQA is live. It's a learning platform built for developers and testers who want to stop guessing and start building reliable, maintainable test suites.

What's inside: hands-on coding labs, test automation courses, and career-focused content designed around what employers actually look for — not theoretical fluff.

I built this because most QA education online is either outdated, surface-level, or buried behind bloated learning management systems. This is my attempt to do it better.

Still early, still iterating. But the foundation is solid and the first courses are ready.

Check it out and let me know what you'd want to see next: https://ultimateqawebsite.vercel.app
