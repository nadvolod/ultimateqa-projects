# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Portfolio

After months of late nights and countless iterations, I'm thrilled to share something I built for someone whose story deserves to be told well.

This is Carmen Ramirez's personal brand portfolio — a fast, modern site built with Next.js App Router and deployed on Vercel. The goal was simple: give her professional identity a home on the web that actually feels like her.

Under the hood: optimized font loading so nothing feels sluggish, blob storage ready for future media needs, and the kind of performance you get when you stop overthinking and just ship on Vercel.

If you're a developer thinking about helping someone you admire get their corner of the internet right — just start. The tech stack almost doesn't matter. What matters is that the story lands.
