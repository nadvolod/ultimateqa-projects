# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Portfolio Site

After a few focused sessions, Carmen Ramirez now has a personal portfolio site that actually does her work justice.

Built it on Next.js and deployed to Vercel — the combination makes performance optimization almost effortless. Fonts load fast, pages feel instant, and the whole thing stays out of the way so Carmen's projects take center stage.

The goal was simple: give her a credible, professional home on the web that she controls. No template rigidity, no platform lock-in, just a clean foundation she can grow into.

If you're a freelancer or independent professional still relying on LinkedIn as your only portfolio, this is the kind of thing worth investing a weekend into.

Live site: https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app
