# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox

I've been wanting a dedicated space to run chaotic UI experiments without worrying about breaking anything important. So I built one.

Nexus Games Workshop Chaos Sandbox is a browser-based playground designed for exactly that — running interactive demos, testing edge cases, and exploring how interfaces behave under pressure. No setup, no local environment needed.

It's been useful for workshop sessions where I want to show real scenarios without putting production systems at risk. Educators and developers can drop participants into the sandbox and let them break things intentionally.

Deployed instantly on Vercel, open source, and ready to fork for your own experiments.

Try it live: https://nexus-games-seven-jade.vercel.app
Source: https://github.com/nadvolod/nexus-games
