# LinkedIn — Note My Life - Capture Your Hard-Earned Wisdom

I've been building something personal, and it's finally live.

Note My Life started from a frustration I kept running into: I'd learn something the hard way, feel the weight of it in the moment, and then slowly forget it. Weeks later, I'd make the same mistake again. The lesson was never really captured — just felt and lost.

So I built a journaling app specifically for life lessons and insights. Not a diary, not a task manager. A place where hard-earned wisdom actually lives, stays searchable, and doesn't fade.

The core idea is simple: when something teaches you something real, you should be able to record it in seconds and find it again before history repeats itself. That's it. Quick capture, searchable journal, preserved insights.

It's built for people who reflect on their experiences and want those reflections to actually compound over time — not just disappear into a notebook or a forgotten notes app.

If that resonates, I'd love for you to try it and tell me what's missing: https://notemylife-v2.vercel.app — source is open at https://github.com/nadvolod/LifeNotes
