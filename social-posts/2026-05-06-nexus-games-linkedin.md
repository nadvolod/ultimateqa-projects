# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Sandbox Playground

After spending time watching QA engineers struggle to find realistic practice environments, I built something to fix that.

Nexus Games is a sandbox playground designed specifically for developers and testers who want to sharpen their skills against genuinely chaotic UI scenarios — not sanitized demos, but the kind of unpredictable behavior you actually encounter in production.

It's workshop-ready out of the box. Whether you're running a testing workshop or just want a personal playground to experiment with new QA strategies, you can jump straight in without setup friction.

The goal was simple: give people a realistic environment where breaking things is the point. QA skill-building shouldn't require access to a fragile production system or a perfectly staged demo app.

Live now on Vercel — try it yourself: https://nexus-games-seven-jade.vercel.app

Source is fully open: https://github.com/nadvolod/nexus-games — contributions and feedback welcome.
