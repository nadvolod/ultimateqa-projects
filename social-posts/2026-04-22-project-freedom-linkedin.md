# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

We started with a simple observation: access to effective, physician-supervised weight management care is still unnecessarily hard for most people. Insurance hoops, long waits, fragmented follow-up. We wanted to fix the access layer.

KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians who can prescribe GLP-1 therapies, handles the telehealth consultation end-to-end, and delivers medication directly to the patient's door. Clinical-grade care without the friction.

This is built for people who are serious about their health and want a streamlined, trustworthy path to treatment — not another wellness app with vague promises.

We're early, we're iterating fast, and we'd genuinely value feedback from anyone in the health-tech, telehealth, or patient advocacy space. What are we missing? What would make this better?
