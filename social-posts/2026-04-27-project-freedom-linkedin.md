# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

The problem we kept running into: people who genuinely need GLP-1 treatments can't easily access them. The process is fragmented — find a doctor, navigate insurance, track down a pharmacy, repeat. We wanted to remove every unnecessary step from that journey.

KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians who can prescribe GLP-1 medications, then ships treatment directly to their door. The entire workflow runs through telehealth, so there's no waiting room, no runaround.

This isn't a supplement store or a workaround. It's physician-prescribed, medically supervised care — just delivered in a way that actually fits how people live in 2024.

We have a lot still to build, but the core experience is working and we're ready for real patients. If you or someone you know has been trying to navigate this space, I'd genuinely appreciate you checking it out.
