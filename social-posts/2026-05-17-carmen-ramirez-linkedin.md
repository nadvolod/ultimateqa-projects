# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Portfolio

I just shipped something I've been meaning to do for a while — a proper home on the internet for Carmen Ramirez.

Built with Next.js and deployed on Vercel's edge network, the goal was simple: fast, clean, and actually representative of the work behind the name. No bloat, no templates that look like everyone else's.

A few things I focused on that don't always get attention: font loading performance, iterative editing speed, and making sure the site felt snappy on any device, anywhere in the world. Small details, but they add up.

If you're curious, the live site is at https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app and the source is open at https://github.com/nadvolod/carmen-ramirez. Take a look, fork it, or just steal the font setup — no judgment.

Your personal brand deserves infrastructure that matches your work. This is what that looks like.
