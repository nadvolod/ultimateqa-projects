# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand & Portfolio Site

Shipped something I'm proud of this week — Carmen Ramirez's personal brand and portfolio site is now live.

Built on Next.js and deployed to Vercel, the goal was simple: give Carmen a fast, clean digital home that actually represents her professionally. No bloat, no unnecessary complexity — just a site that loads quickly and gets out of the way.

Typography and performance were treated as first-class concerns from the start. A slow or visually inconsistent personal site undermines the very brand it's supposed to build.

If you're curious about the implementation, the source is open: https://github.com/nadvolod/carmen-ramirez

And the live site: https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app
