# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Portfolio

After months of iterating on how to present Carmen's work and story online, the site is finally live.

Built with Next.js and deployed on Vercel's edge network, it loads fast and stays out of the way — letting the work speak for itself. Optimized font loading, clean layout, and a foundation that's ready for dynamic content as Carmen's story keeps evolving.

The goal was never to build something flashy. It was to build something honest — a corner of the internet that genuinely reflects who she is and what she brings to collaborators and clients.

If you're curious, take a look: https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app

Source is open if you want to poke around the implementation: https://github.com/nadvolod/carmen-ramirez
