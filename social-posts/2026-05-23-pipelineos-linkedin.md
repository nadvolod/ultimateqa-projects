# LinkedIn — PipelineOS - Founder Voice Sales Agent for LinkedIn

I've been thinking about this problem for a while: most LinkedIn outreach tools make you sound like everyone else. The same templates, the same openers, the same robotic energy that gets ignored.

So I built PipelineOS — a sales agent that learns how you actually write and sends outbound messages that sound like you, not like a sequence tool.

The core idea is simple. Early-stage founders don't have time to personally message 50 prospects a day, but they also can't afford to sound like a BDR running a cadence. PipelineOS sits in the middle — automating the volume while preserving the voice that makes founders credible.

Right now it handles LinkedIn outbound with personalized AI-generated messages trained on your writing style. No templates you have to awkwardly edit. No 'I came across your profile and was impressed' openers.

If you're a founder doing your own outbound or a small sales team trying to scale without losing authenticity, I'd love for you to try it and tell me what's broken.
