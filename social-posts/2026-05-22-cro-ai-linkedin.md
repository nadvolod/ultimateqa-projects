# LinkedIn — CRO Agent - AI-Powered Revenue Optimization

I've been thinking about a problem that keeps coming up for early-stage founders: you need a Chief Revenue Officer's perspective, but you don't have one on the team yet.

So I built CRO Agent — an AI that analyzes your product portfolio and surfaces revenue optimization strategies the way a seasoned CRO would. Not generic advice. Actual analysis of what you have and where the growth levers are.

The core idea is simple: most founders are making revenue decisions with incomplete information and no dedicated expert to pressure-test their thinking. Growth teams are stretched thin. CRO Agent steps in to fill that gap — running portfolio analysis, identifying underperforming areas, and generating strategy recommendations without the six-figure salary.

It's early, and I'm still refining the model based on real use cases. But if you're a founder or growth lead who wants to stress-test your revenue strategy, I'd genuinely love your feedback.
