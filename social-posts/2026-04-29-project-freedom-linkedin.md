# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

The problem we kept hearing: people who want GLP-1 treatment spend weeks navigating referrals, insurance walls, and appointment backlogs — only to give up before they start. The system wasn't built for them.

KratosRx fixes that. Physician-prescribed GLP-1 therapy, telehealth-enabled from day one, delivered directly to your door. No gatekeeping. No unnecessary friction.

We obsessed over the onboarding experience because that's where most patients lose confidence. If the first five minutes feel clinical and cold, you've already lost them. We built something that meets people where they are.

This is still early. We're learning, iterating, and talking to patients every day. If you know someone who's been putting off getting care because the process felt too complicated, send them here: https://www.kratosrx.com

Feedback from anyone in the healthcare or product space is genuinely welcome.
