# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

The problem we kept hearing: patients who could genuinely benefit from GLP-1 treatments were stuck navigating fragmented care — confusing insurance hurdles, long waits, and no clear path forward. We decided to fix the access layer.

KratosRx connects patients directly with physicians who can evaluate, prescribe, and ship GLP-1 treatments to their door. The entire experience is telehealth-powered, so geography and scheduling friction stop being blockers to real metabolic health care.

We spent a lot of time on the onboarding flow. Most health platforms lose patients in the first five minutes. We rebuilt that experience from the ground up with one goal: make it feel like someone is actually guiding you, not processing you.

This is early. There's a lot still to build. But the core is working, physicians are on the platform, and patients are getting care they couldn't easily access before.
