# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Website

I built a personal brand website for Carmen Ramirez, and it's now live.

The goal was simple: create a polished, fast, modern web presence that actually reflects someone's professional identity — without the bloat of page builders or weeks of back-and-forth.

Built with Next.js and deployed on Vercel, the site loads fast, looks sharp on any device, and was ready to ship in minutes rather than months. Modern typography, clean layout, nothing unnecessary.

If you're an individual looking to establish a serious online presence, this is the kind of foundation worth starting from.

Live site: https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app
