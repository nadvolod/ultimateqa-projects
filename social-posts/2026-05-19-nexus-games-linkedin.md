# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox

I built a sandbox and called it Workshop Chaos — because that's exactly what it is.

Nexus Games is a live playground for developers and testers who want to explore chaotic game mechanics without spinning up local environments or writing setup scripts. You open the URL and start breaking things immediately.

The core idea came from running workshops where I kept watching people spend the first 20 minutes just getting their environment working. That's dead time. This removes that friction entirely — it's Vercel-deployed, always live, always ready.

The chaos mechanics aren't a gimmick. They're intentional tools for testing how interactive systems behave under unpredictable conditions. If you're building games, simulations, or just want a weird environment to experiment in, this is the kind of playground that doesn't judge your edge cases.

It's open source. The repo is at https://github.com/nadvolod/nexus-games if you want to poke around the internals or contribute.

Go break something at https://nexus-games-seven-jade.vercel.app
