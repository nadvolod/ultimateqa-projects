# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox

I built something a little different this time — not a polished product, but a deliberate mess.

Nexus Games: Workshop Chaos Sandbox is a sandbox environment where developers and testers can explore chaotic UI scenarios and edge cases without worrying about breaking anything real. It's the kind of tool I kept wishing existed every time I was running a workshop or trying to demo edge case testing strategies.

The idea is simple: give people a controlled space to experience the chaos, learn from it, and build better instincts for handling it in production.

It's got interactive chaos scenarios, a range of edge cases to poke at, and it's designed to be dropped straight into a workshop or team session without any setup friction.

If you run testing workshops, mentor QA engineers, or just like breaking things responsibly — this might be useful to you.
