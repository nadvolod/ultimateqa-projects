# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

We set out to solve a genuinely frustrating problem: access to physician-prescribed GLP-1 therapy is fragmented, confusing, and often gatekept by systems that weren't designed with the patient in mind.

KratosRx changes that. Patients connect with a licensed physician, get a personalized GLP-1 weight management plan, and have their medication delivered directly to their door — all through a telehealth-first experience built around how people actually want to manage their health in 2024.

The onboarding is intentionally simple. No unnecessary friction, no confusing portals. Just a clear path from "I'm ready" to "my treatment is on the way."

This is early, and there's a lot still to build. But the foundation is solid and the problem is real.
