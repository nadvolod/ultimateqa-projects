# LinkedIn — Carmen Ramirez - Personal Brand Portfolio

After months of thinking about it, Carmen Ramirez finally has a corner of the internet that's entirely her own.

Built with Next.js and deployed on Vercel, the site is fast, clean, and built to grow with her. Optimized typography, dynamic content support, and a design that stays out of the way so her work can speak.

Sometimes the best thing you can do for someone is help them show up online the way they deserve to.

If you want to see what a focused, no-fluff personal portfolio looks like: https://carmen-ramirez.vercel.app

Source is open if you're curious how it's put together: https://github.com/nadvolod/carmen-ramirez
