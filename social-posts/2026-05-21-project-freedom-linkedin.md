# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

The problem we kept hearing: people want access to GLP-1 treatments but the path there is fragmented — confusing consults, unclear pricing, and logistics that feel stuck in 2005. We built something cleaner.

KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians through a streamlined telehealth flow, and if prescribed, medication ships directly to their door. The whole experience is designed around reducing friction for people who are serious about their health.

What we focused on technically: a consult flow that doesn't feel like a form dump, onboarding that actually prepares patients for what to expect, and a backend that handles the coordination between clinical and fulfillment without the patient ever feeling that complexity.

This is early. There's a lot we want to improve. But the core loop works and real patients are moving through it.
