# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, we're launching KratosRx today.

The problem we kept hearing: people who qualify for GLP-1 treatments are stuck navigating referrals, waitlists, and pharmacy delays. The gap between 'I want to address this' and 'I actually have my medication' is enormous — and mostly unnecessary.

KratosRx closes that gap. Patients connect with a physician online, get a legitimate prescription if appropriate, and receive their treatment shipped directly to their door. No driving across town. No sitting in waiting rooms. No wondering where things stand.

We built the onboarding to be genuinely fast. The telehealth layer is real clinical care, not a checkbox. And the whole thing is designed around the patient experience, not the system's convenience.

This is early. We have a lot to learn and a lot to build. But the first version is live and we're ready to hear what works and what doesn't.
