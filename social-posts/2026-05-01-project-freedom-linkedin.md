# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

GLP-1 medications have been genuinely transformative for people struggling with weight loss — but access has been a mess. Confusing insurance hurdles, long waits to see a specialist, pharmacy runs that feel like a second job. We built KratosRx to remove as much of that friction as possible.

The platform connects patients with licensed physicians who can prescribe GLP-1 treatments, and delivers medication directly to their door. The whole experience is telehealth-first — no waiting rooms, no unnecessary steps, no gatekeeping.

What we cared most about when building this: putting the patient in control. Your care journey, your pace, your terms.

If you know someone who's been trying to navigate this space and hitting walls, send them to https://www.kratosrx.com. And if you're a builder or clinician interested in where we're taking this, I'd genuinely love to talk.
