# LinkedIn — Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox

I built a sandbox environment for developers and QA engineers who want to practice testing in something that actually feels real.

Nexus Games - Workshop Chaos Sandbox is a game-themed app designed specifically for hands-on learning. Instead of synthetic todo lists or blank forms, you get a UI with actual complexity — the kind of chaos that makes testing interesting and skill-building meaningful.

It's built for workshops, team sessions, or solo practice. Spin it up, break things intentionally, explore edge cases, and sharpen your automation or exploratory testing instincts in a controlled environment.

Fully open source, deployed on Vercel, and ready to use without any setup friction.

If you run QA workshops, onboard new engineers, or just want a more engaging playground for testing practice — this might be worth a look.
