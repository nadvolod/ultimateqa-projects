# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

GLP-1 medications have shown real clinical results for weight management — but access has been a mess. Insurance hurdles, long waits for in-person appointments, pharmacy shortages. We wanted to fix the access layer.

KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians who can prescribe GLP-1 therapy online, with treatment delivered directly to their door. The whole flow — consultation, prescription, personalized care plan, ongoing support — happens through the platform. No waiting rooms, no runaround.

This is medically supervised care, not a supplement storefront. Every patient goes through a real physician consultation. That distinction matters to us.

We're early, but the infrastructure is solid and we're ready to start helping people. If you know someone who's been trying to navigate this space, send them our way.
