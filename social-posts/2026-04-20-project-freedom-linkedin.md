# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care, Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

The problem we kept hearing: people who genuinely want to explore GLP-1 therapy can't easily access it. Long waits, unclear processes, fragmented care. The system wasn't built for them.

So we built something that was. KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians through telehealth, handles the prescription process, and ships treatment directly to their door. The whole thing is designed around the patient's experience, not the clinic's convenience.

We focused hard on onboarding. First impressions in healthcare are everything — if someone feels confused or judged in the first five minutes, they're gone. We wanted the opposite of that.

This is early days. We're still learning, still iterating. But the infrastructure is real, the clinical partnerships are real, and the need is real.
