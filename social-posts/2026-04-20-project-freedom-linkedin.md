# LinkedIn — KratosRx - Physician-Prescribed GLP-1 Care Delivered

After months of building, KratosRx is live.

GLP-1 medications have been a genuine breakthrough for metabolic health — but access has been fragmented. Insurance walls, long waits, and inconsistent follow-up have kept a lot of people from getting real clinical support. We wanted to fix that.

KratosRx connects patients with licensed physicians who prescribe GLP-1 treatment plans tailored to them, with medication shipped directly to their door. The whole onboarding experience runs through telehealth, so there's no waiting room, no referral chain, just care that fits into your actual life.

This is still early days. We're focused on making the patient experience feel supported and human, not like a transaction. If you or someone you know has been looking for a serious, clinically grounded path to weight management, this is built for that.
